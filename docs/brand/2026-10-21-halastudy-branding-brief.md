# Halastudy branding brief and missing assets

Prepared for brand design and product handoff on 2026-10-21. Owner: Aziz Al Khunizan.

**Direction: finish the warm, quiet Halastudy identity already in use.** Build a consistent logo system, bilingual messaging and a small launch kit around it. The visual direction is established; final identity assets and usage rules still need finishing.

This is a design brief, not a completed logo package. Naming, copy and production choices described as working defaults below are recommendations for this handoff, not newly approved brand decisions.

## Product and audience

Halastudy is an Arabic-first web product for quiet shared study. People open or join a room, use their cameras for a sense of company, keep microphones off by default and work alongside one another with a focus timer. Explain body doubling in those everyday terms before using the category name.

**Primary audience:** Saudi and GCC university students aged 18+, especially people facing a difficult first step, a long revision session or a lonely evening at their desk. **Secondary audience:** adults studying for professional qualifications or doing focused independent work. University study leads the message; professional work can appear in supporting examples.

The older May brief included ages 16-17. Current [public terms](https://halastudy.com/terms.html) restrict the service to 18+. Keep recruitment copy and imagery consistent with that boundary.

**The moment to design for:** "I know what I need to do. I just need a place to sit down and start alongside someone."

**Functional promise:** a simple way to open a quiet study room and invite someone. **Emotional promise:** company without pressure to perform or make conversation. These are positioning choices, not measured learning or health outcomes.

## Positioning and name

**Positioning statement:** For adult Saudi and GCC students who find studying alone difficult, Halastudy is an Arabic-first shared study room that makes starting feel less solitary through quiet presence and a simple focus routine.

**Brand idea:** a study lamp on a desk at 1am: warm, focused and yours.

Use these working naming rules until the final identity is signed off:

| Context | Working default |
|---|---|
| Product name in English | Halastudy, one word, sentence case |
| Product name in Arabic prose | هلاستدي |
| Compact symbol | Existing هلا glyph, subject to final review |

The product name and compact glyph have different jobs. The glyph does not replace the full name in explanatory copy. Do not introduce new spacing, transliterations or sub-brands across assets.

## Personality and voice

Sound like a capable friend at the late-night library: warm, calm and brief. Treat students as adults. Welcome someone back without making them explain an absence or a broken streak.

Write Arabic first for Gulf campaigns, using natural Saudi wording, then adapt the English. Use 0-9 digits in both languages for this new kit. Keep room codes and URLs in their normal left-to-right order. The digit rule is a handoff default and needs reconciliation with older guidance that allowed Eastern Arabic digits.

| Use | English draft | Arabic draft |
|---|---|---|
| Short descriptor | Quiet study rooms, together. | غرف مذاكرة هادية، مع غيرك. |
| Main headline | A quiet room. A little company. | غرفة هادية. وأحد يذاكر معك. |
| Product explanation | Open a room, invite a study partner and start your timer. | افتح غرفة، اعزم أحد يذاكر معك، وشغل المؤقت. |
| Primary action | Open a room | افتح غرفة |
| Join action | Join a room | ادخل غرفة |
| Empty room | You're the first here. Invite someone to join. | أنت أول واحد هنا. اعزم أحد يذاكر معك. |

These are proposed copy samples, not deployed wording. Keep praise quiet and specific. Remove guilt, hustle slogans, superlatives and promises of guaranteed grades or concentration. Use ordinary language about studying together; make no treatment claims.

## Visual direction to retain

Use warm paper, dark ink and one karak amber accent. Give text and controls room to breathe. Keep modern Gulf character subtle through Arabic craft, light and material choices.

| Role | Existing color | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | #F5F2EB | Main light background |
| Ink | #2D3436 | Main text |
| Karak amber | #E0B08B | Signature fill or restrained highlight |
| Night sky | #0E1218 | Main dark background |
| Dark card | #1B2230 | Raised dark surface |
| Warm light ink | #ECE6D8 | Main dark-mode text |

Retain Newsreader for Latin display, Inter for Latin UI, Tajawal for Arabic UI, Amiri for Arabic display and JetBrains Mono for timers and codes. Package font sources, weights, licenses and fallback rules in the final handoff. Keep Lucide as the working icon family.

Use one clear headline and one main action per asset. Respect Arabic reading order, preserve English parity and provide quiet motion with a reduced-motion version. Use original, licensed desk or study imagery with warm light. Keep new drafts focused on objects, hands or consenting adult subjects with documented usage rights.

The May imagery guidance restricts women's faces without specific approval, while current promotional assets include female-presenting subjects. Preserve existing files; resolve approval and usage rights before choosing material for the final kit. Never use participant camera feeds as marketing material.

### Contrast findings

Calculated from opaque hexadecimal color pairs in the existing tokens and design recipes. These are pair-level findings, not a rendered audit of every screen. The [W3C contrast guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html) sets a 4.5:1 target for ordinary text and 3:1 for large text, with defined exceptions including logotypes.

| Foreground on background | Calculated ratio | Ordinary text |
|---|---|---|
| #A56B43 on #F5F2EB | 3.926:1 | Below target |
| #9AA0A4 on #F5F2EB | 2.366:1 | Below target |
| #FAF7F1 on #C28A62 | 2.764:1 | Below target |
| #2D3436 on #E0B08B | 6.493:1 | Meets target |

Keep the signature amber as a fill or highlight and use darker text where needed. For example, proposed brown #93603C on paper #F5F2EB computes to 4.721:1. Validate all actual states before adopting a new token; no product colors were changed for this brief.

## Designer deliverables

Finish one cohesive direction using the existing identity. Start with the bilingual wordmark and compact symbol, then prove them in a header, a social avatar and a WhatsApp invite before building the rest.

| Deliverable | Required specification |
|---|---|
| Identity masters | Editable SVG masters and an organized Figma source file; wordmark, symbol and bilingual lockup; full-color, dark-background and one-color exports; transparent PNGs with 2000px longest edge. |
| Usage sheet | Minimum sizes, clear space, symbol versus full-name use, background rules and examples of incorrect usage. Test the symbol at 16px, 32px and in a circular crop. |
| Font and color pack | Type styles, selected weights, font-license/source list, light/dark tokens and measured text/control pairings. |
| Avatar and website icons | 1024x1024 social master; favicon and PWA exports reconciled with existing files and the manifest. These formats already exist; review and unify them. |
| Link preview | 1200x630 template with Arabic and English versions, legible name and a clear descriptor. |
| Portrait post | 1080x1350 editable template for one message and one action. |
| Story and invite | 1080x1920 story plus 1080x1350 WhatsApp invite. Include room title, start time with timezone, duration and share link; use 1080x1080 only as an additional crop if needed. |
| Messaging sheet | Final bilingual copy for the product descriptor, hero, explanation, CTA pair, short profile bio and 3 key interaction states. |
| Asset index | One README with current filenames, version, owner, approval state and usage rights. Include light/dark examples and export presets. |

Native app launch art, a mascot, a bespoke icon family and a full campaign calendar are outside this first kit. Keep the current web product and working icon family as the design basis.

## Acceptance and release

- A new adult student can explain what Halastudy does, who it is for and how to start after seeing the first screen.
- English and Arabic assets use the same name rules and feel equally finished. Arabic is shaped correctly, has no tashkeel and preserves LTR links and room codes.
- The wordmark is legible at its specified minimum size; the symbol works at favicon sizes and under a circular crop.
- Normal text, large text and meaningful controls meet their applicable contrast targets. Semantic states also use words or shapes.
- Previews are labeled as previews. No invented activity, testimonials, clinical results or guaranteed outcomes appear in the kit.
- Camera visibility and microphone defaults are explained plainly. A platform recording policy must never be phrased as a guarantee that another participant cannot capture a screen.
- Public wording matches actual deployed features. The live terms' older transport wording differs from the candidate's RealtimeKit wording; resolve that release/documentation issue separately before making technical privacy claims.
- Editable files, exports, font licenses and image permissions are indexed. Final artwork remains marked draft until Aziz approves it.

**Best next production action:** finalize the existing Halastudy wordmark and هلا symbol together, with a bilingual lockup, minimum-size checks and a contrast-safe usage sheet. Those choices unlock the rest of the template kit.
