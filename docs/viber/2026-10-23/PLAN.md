# Halastudy production gate — 2026-10-23

1. Map: baseline, trust boundaries, risky surface and release requirements.
2. Boot: tracked-source archive, isolated synthetic backend, frozen install and full CI.
3. Trace: enumerate surfaces and follow success/failure contracts.
4. Probe: anonymous, malformed, cross-account, concurrency and browser journeys.
5. Sweep: guard twins, secrets, async failures and shipped-source parity.
6. Visitor: local shipped artifact and at most 30 anonymous production GET/HEAD requests.
7. Secure: authorization, privacy, supply chain and recovery evidence.
8. Fix: reproduce, patch and commit each eligible concern separately.
9. Decide: final verification, exact coverage and owner acceptance batch.

The two read-heavy traces run in parallel as required by the invoked viber skill.
All test data and providers are synthetic. No push, merge, deployment, live writes,
messages, paid calls or provider settings are authorized.
