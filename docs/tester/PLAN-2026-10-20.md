Make first-time study and returning study obvious in Arabic and English: understand -> start/join -> confirm -> focus -> recap -> return. Preserve existing user work. The checkout currently contains unrelated dirty DigetPay changes; do not overwrite, reset or fold those into a UX patch.

## Scope and keep

- Preserve warm paper/amber styling, Arabic-first content, guest study, camera-optional operation, real room-code handoff and free core tools.
- Preserve server-verified focus credits, membership enforcement, sandbox notices, disabled unconfigured checkout and existing billing reconciliation behavior.
- Local screenshots are evidence of a candidate. `/app`, `/plans` and `/recover` returned 404 on live production on 2026-10-20.
- Do not fabricate presence counts, imply real student endorsements, reveal private rooms or claim auto-renewal.
- No deployment or payment-provider mutation belongs in this UX implementation.
- Use logical CSS, named controls, keyboard support, Arabic and English, light and dark, and widths 390, 844 and 1440.
- Effort: S = bounded copy/state/control fix; M = one flow or component; L = multi-surface layout/state work. These are relative sizes, not delivery promises.
