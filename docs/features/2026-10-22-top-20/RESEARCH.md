# Research and feature selection

Checked official product sources on 2026-10-22. Raw domain-restricted Tavily results are retained in research/. These are twenty selected additions suited to Halastudy, not a claim of market-share ranking or feature parity with every competitor.

| Official source | Observed pattern | Halastudy adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| [Focus To-Do](https://www.focustodo.cn/) | Tasks, projects, subtasks, due dates, repeat rules, estimates, reminders and time reports | F01–F08: a private planner, explicit session linkage, and saved routines. Task completion grants no XP. |
| [Focusmate features](https://www.focusmate.com/features) and [favorite partners](https://support.focusmate.com/en/articles/6365663-favorite-partners) | Predictable focus durations, familiar company, task modes and calendar integrations | F15: save accessible rooms rather than introduce public matching or camera requirements. F18: private calendar download. |
| [Flow Club: how it works](https://www.flow.club/how-it-works) | Scheduled company, intentions, session structure and closing check-ins | F07, F11, F16–F17: task-linked focus, private reflections, a cross-circle agenda and voluntary RSVPs. |
| [Caveday](https://www.caveday.org/) and [FAQ](https://www.caveday.org/faq) | Guided deep-work rounds with breaks and closing reflection | F09–F11: finite routines, parked distractions and a quiet reflection. Each next round requires an explicit choice. |
| [Study Together study rooms](https://www.studytogether.com/zoom) | Quiet timed study/break spaces and limited distractions | F12–F14, F19–F20: personal targets and useful private history, optional reminders and a small circle resource shelf; preserve quiet participation. |

F01–F20 and exact acceptance criteria are in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md). Existing room boards, ambient audio, readiness signals, timer presets, seven-day activity, recurring room schedules and individual calendar links are not counted as new features.

Selection favors one useful loop: plan a small task, choose a rhythm, focus with trusted accounting, reflect, and return to a familiar place or appointment. Public feeds, rankings, compulsory cameras, automatic matching, external messaging and paid infrastructure are outside this implementation.
