## Reviewed improvements that are present

- The copy extraction preserves the established translation contract: `platform-copy.js` initializes the Arabic base from parsed `data-lang` nodes and adds both language dictionaries before the deferred controller (`platform.html:15-17`). New home, rhythm, invitation, privacy and room-hint strings have AR/EN entries (`platform-copy.js:233-255`). No missing core translation key was identified by the scoped read.
- Safe-return handling is shared by password authentication and OAuth (`account.html:503-508`; `services/auth/google-routes.js:80-83,195-225`). OAuth success uses its nonce-bound validated destination. Known-flow exchange/missing-code failure retains `next` (`google-routes.js:104-126`); account only scrubs `auth`, preserving query/hash (`account.html:480-484`). Delete/security purposes keep their dedicated destinations (`google-routes.js:164-168`). State-mismatch/expired-flow recovery must remain cautious rather than trusting a callback’s arbitrary `next`.
- The invitation flow now creates a copyable same-origin fragment link, leaves acceptance explicit after identity, keeps the token on rejected acceptance, revokes unused invitations with confirmation, and clears fragment on accepted membership (`platform.js:515-543`). DX-01 is its same-document/draft lifecycle gap, not a reason to remove the handoff.
- Member room focus now has a separate controller that restores canonical current state and exposes acknowledged credit/recap. Legacy `/api/me/focus-session` submission was removed from countdown completion, and account history is clearly labeled as legacy/imported. This addresses the previous untrusted history presentation without deleting data.
- Room readiness reuses existing status rather than introducing another social system (`index.html:2690-2706,4063-4067`); sharing-off remains explained. Shared goal is rendered with textContent and `bdi dir=auto` (`index.html:807,3403-3404`). Actual presence produces alone/together/connecting copy (`3643-3645`), without fabricated people.
- Home return, next-session and owned-room sections are truly conditional (`platform.js:130-148`); the start action and quiet company choices remain available in the empty member state. The mobile navigation retains four destinations, and existing title/current-page/history/heading-focus behavior remains intact.
- Lobby returning-name handlers are now installed for both fresh and returning visitors (`lobby.html:530-541`); density resets before rebuilding (`269-270`). Camera labels derive from actual SDK enabled state, have `aria-pressed`, remove stale guest translation binding, and rerender on language change (`319-320,451-465,536`). The original inert returning-user gate and mislabeled on/off action are corrected in source.
